Our Story & Ethos

Providing Safety, Fostering Intellect

Founded in 2014, The Mother Lap School in Peshawar has spent over a decade establishing a learning ecosystem where young pupils feel protected, cherished, and challenged to achieve academic greatness.

Our specialized separation policies for boys and girls support cultural expectations perfectly while providing highly focused classroom instruction.

Our Core Pillars

Integrity & Morals: Instilling traditional Islamic and cultural values daily.

Science-Focused Curricula: Cutting-edge visual instruction and early coding habits.

Constant Supervision: Campus entry and exit checkpoints to ensure absolute parent peace of mind.

Parents FAQ

Where are the branches located?

Our campuses are located across Muzammil Town (which includes 3 separate wings for absolute protection), Wazir Colony, and Qazi Abad in Peshawar.

Are the Boys & Girls campus locations fully apart?

Yes. For grades 4th to 10th, we maintain completely distinct physical spaces with dedicated faculty to ensure traditional security parameters.
